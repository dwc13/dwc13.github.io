## Overview

A research-driven design project examining how queer individuals represent and present their identities on digital dating platforms.

## Research Findings

- Identity representation challenges and opportunities
- Safety and privacy concerns in digital dating
- Community and belonging in digital spaces
- Inclusivity gaps in current platform designs

## Design Solutions

- Expanded identity category options
- Safety-first privacy controls
- Community features and verification
- Accessibility improvements

## Prototypes & Testing

Created interactive prototypes tested with 100+ queer individuals across different demographics. Iteratively refined based on user feedback and insights.

## Publications

Research findings published in peer-reviewed journals and presented at design and technology conferences.
