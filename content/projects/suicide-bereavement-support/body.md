## Overview

A comprehensive design research and digital platform dedicated to providing support for individuals grieving the loss of a loved one to suicide.

## Project Goals

- Create a compassionate, accessible platform for grief support
- Develop a complete design system for consistent user experience
- Research and implement best practices for mental health digital products

## Design Process

### Research Phase

Conducted interviews with grief counselors and bereaved individuals to understand needs and pain points.

### Design Phase

- Created wireframes and user flows
- Developed high-fidelity mockups
- Built interactive prototypes

## Outcomes

- Complete design system with 40+ components
- Responsive web interface tested with 50+ users
- Published findings in design research
- Platform launched with 1000+ active users
