## Overview

A healthcare design initiative focused on creating compassionate digital experiences for end-of-life care, patient communication, and family support.

## Challenge

Healthcare digital products often fail to address the emotional and relational aspects of end-of-life care. This project prioritizes compassion, dignity, and human connection.

## User Research

- Interviews with hospice patients and families
- Observations in healthcare settings
- Conversations with medical professionals
- Sensitivity and ethics training

## Design Principles

- Dignity and respect in every interaction
- Clear, compassionate communication
- Support for family involvement
- Accessibility for diverse users

## Implementation

Designed and tested a compassionate interface for patient portals, family communication tools, and medical information sharing. Currently deployed in 15 healthcare facilities.
