## Overview

A multidisciplinary study exploring how individuals and communities engage with death and mourning through social networks and digital memorialization.

## Research Questions

- How do digital platforms shape mourning practices?
- What role do social networks play in collective grief?
- How can design honor memorialization?
- What are the ethical considerations in digital death?

## Methodology

- Qualitative interviews with grieving individuals
- Social media analysis and documentation
- Design workshops and co-creation sessions
- Interactive visual documentation

## Visual Documentation

Created a series of interactive digital experiences and visual installations that explore themes of digital mourning, memory, and collective grief.

## Outcomes

Published research that has influenced social media platform policies around memorialization and digital legacy.
