## Overview

A foundational research project exploring queer design principles and how human-computer interaction can be more inclusive, equitable, and affirming of queer experiences.

## Core Research Areas

- Queer theory in design practice
- Intersectionality and digital inclusion
- Non-normative interfaces and experiences
- Resisting binaries in design

## Methodology

Developed a research framework combining queer theory, participatory design, and critical design. Conducted workshops with queer designers, researchers, and communities.

## Key Findings

- Traditional HCI methods may reinforce heteronormative structures
- Queer design practices can expand possibilities for all users
- Participatory approaches center marginalized voices
- Intersectionality must inform design decisions

## Publications & Impact

Published peer-reviewed papers, gave keynote talks at major conferences, and influenced design practices in tech companies and design schools globally.
