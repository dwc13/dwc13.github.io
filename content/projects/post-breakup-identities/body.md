## Overview

An in-depth visual exploration of identity transformation and reinvention following romantic separation.

## Concept

This project examines how individuals reconstruct their sense of self after the end of significant relationships. Through visual storytelling and brand identity design, we document the journey of transformation.

## Visual Approach

- Color psychology and symbolism
- Typography as self-expression
- Photography and visual metaphors
- Interactive digital experiences

## Deliverables

- Complete brand identity system
- Visual documentation series
- Interactive web experience
- Print materials and publications

## Impact

The project has been featured in design publications and has resonated with audiences exploring themes of identity and change. It opens conversations about personal growth and reinvention.
